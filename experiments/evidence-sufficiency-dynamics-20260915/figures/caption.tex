\textbf{Native sufficiency around first full evidence coverage.}
Natural Qwen3.6-27B trajectories are aligned to the first decision state at which all annotated gold evidence is retained ($t_R$). The line shows the equal-weight mean across questions and the band shows a 95\% question-cluster bootstrap interval. Values are computed only from observed states, without padding, smoothing, or a monotonicity constraint; the number of contributing questions is shown below each offset. Sufficiency is already favored before full annotated coverage and peaks when full coverage is first reached.
