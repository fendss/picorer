\textbf{Adding gold evidence increases the model's native sufficiency likelihood.}
Each matched pair holds the question, evidence-slot count, fact order, and all other facts fixed, while replacing one approximately word-length-matched unrelated fact with one missing gold fact in the same slot; this raises gold-evidence coverage by one hop.
Gray lines show the mean paired change for each of 64 predeclared questions without known gold conflicts after averaging all eligible starting subsets and both fact orders within the question; the red line shows the mean across questions.
The mean sufficiency likelihood increases from 0.360 to 0.809, corresponding to a paired increase of 0.449 (95\% question-bootstrap CI: 0.428--0.466).
