# Internal review — not part of the user-facing experimental report

The requested document contains experiments only. Historical narrative, tool citations,
source-path inventories, QA narratives and scheduler records are intentionally excluded
from the presentation, as explicitly requested by the user. Original artifacts remain intact.

## Outline and paragraph roles

1. Setup: task, primary cohort, state timing, R, native margin and likelihood, answer protocol.
2. Natural states: question, trajectory alignment, observed association, time-control limitation.
3. Controlled evidence: question, paired replacement, likelihood and answer effects.
4. Preview: question, same-state manipulation, fixed-R response and behavioral boundary.
5. Prediction: question, question-held-out validation, lack of detected incremental benefit.
6. Sensitivity: all-question comparison, fallback and measurement limits.
7. Conclusions: content sensitivity, visibility/package distinction, limits on readiness/stop claims.

## Five-dimensional review

- Contribution: supports evidence sensitivity and preview sensitivity, not a new stopping policy.
- Clarity: one primary cohort throughout; all-question estimates explicitly identified, no version narrative.
- Strength: paired effects and uncertainty retained; likelihood conditional time-regression instability and predictive null retained.
- Completeness: 100-question sensitivity, 36-question subgroup results, low higher-hop counts, fallback behavior and exact-match caveats retained.
- Soundness: no new inference, no replacement of empirical answers by fitted outcomes; state/trajectory/question weighting distinguished; status probability not action probability.

## Claim–evidence map

- Positive natural association: primary cohort question-weighted correlation. Supported descriptively, not causally.
- Content response: paired standard-fact replacement. Supported in controlled synthetic contexts.
- Package-external influence: relevant/unrelated preview comparison at fixed R. Supported; not evidence of actual early stopping.
- No established incremental prediction benefit: paired held-out loss changes span zero for both scales. Supported as non-detection, not equivalence or universal uselessness.

## Consistency actions

Natural trajectory and coverage figures recomputed from the exact same 64 questions and
1,181 states as intervention/answer primary analyses. Time-control intervals recomputed
with 2,000 whole-question bootstrap draws. Coefficients reproduce the existing estimates.
Temporal/coverage means average runs within question; pooled correlations give each question
equal total weight; trajectory FE gives each of the three runs equal weight. These estimate
different, explicitly stated summaries and are not compared as if interchangeable.
