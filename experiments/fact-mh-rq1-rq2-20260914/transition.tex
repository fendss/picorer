On the 64 FactConsolidation-MH questions without previously identified gold-label conflicts, the final evidence package lacked at least one annotated supporting fact in 21/52 (40.4\%), 47/55 (85.5\%), and 27/44 (61.4\%) of runs explicitly ending with \texttt{sufficient} for Qwen3.6-27B, GPT-4o-mini, and GPT-5-mini, respectively, with one run per question and backbone.
These terminal discrepancies are not visible in aggregate accuracy alone, but do not establish that the model lacked relevant information elsewhere in its context or that further retrieval would improve its answer.
